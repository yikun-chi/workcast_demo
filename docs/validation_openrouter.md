# Validation: AI token demand and spend (OpenRouter)

Numeric claims are generated by `scripts/build_openrouter_validation.py` from `runs/openrouter/evaluation.json`.

Review status: archived results are conditional on later prices and model launches, and app categories use usage across the full collected period. The usage-truncation test does not cover that category construction. Do not interpret these results as accuracy using only information available at the prediction date.

## Design

At each of 16 Monday cutoffs from 2025-07-07 to 2026-08-31, the 100 apps with the most tokens in the previous four weeks are carried forward for 8 weeks. Company dynamics (starting level, forward drift and survival by persistence class, damping, shock sizes and range width) are fitted at each cutoff from backtests whose targets end before it. After the cutoff the simulation receives the published list prices (Internet Archive captures of the OpenRouter model list) and the dates new models were listed. The point prediction is the simulation's central path, with every random shock at its median; ranges are the 5th-95th percentile of 20 runs per cutoff. A test (`tests/test_market.py`) confirms that deleting all usage after a cutoff leaves the prediction unchanged.

Actual spend is each company's tokens times the blended list price of its use-case segment's actual model mix; a company's own model mix is not public. 19.1% of company-weeks after a cutoff fall outside OpenRouter's weekly top 200 and are recorded at half the 200th app's volume on both sides. Cutoffs four weeks apart give overlapping 8-week windows.

Negative error reduction means the simulation loses.

In dollars: the leading companies spent $63.0M per 8-week window on average at list prices. The simulation's spend forecast missed by 15.7% of that and the best standard forecast by 18.9%, so the simulation landed $2.06M closer per window, about $13M a year (6.5 windows). This is forecast error avoided, not spending saved; spend grew over the period, so later windows weigh more in dollars.

## What the lower error is worth to a company

A company that buys committed capacity (reserved units at a discount, as with provisioned-throughput reservations: unused reserved units are lost and use above the reservation pays the pay-as-you-go rate; [mechanics](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/cost-management-billing/reservations/azure-openai)) and commits to a forecast of its 8-week spend loses (1 - discount) on every dollar over-committed and the discount on every dollar it had to buy at the full rate. ## Limitations

This is a retrospective evaluation: the model's structure was chosen with these periods available, so it is not a prospective holdout. Forecasts published before the weeks they predict are the clean test. Usage covers traffic routed through OpenRouter, not the whole market; providers count tokens with different tokenizers; spend is at list prices, and caching and discounts make realised spend lower. The alternative's size depends on an authored price sensitivity (`calibration/market.yaml`). A language-model buyer seat that judges new models is available as a configuration change; its measured result is in `docs/plans/buyer_seat_protocol.md`.
