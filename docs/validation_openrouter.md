# Validation: AI token demand and spend (OpenRouter)

Numeric claims are generated by `scripts/build_openrouter_validation.py` from `runs/openrouter/evaluation.json`.

## Design

At each of 16 Monday cutoffs from 2025-07-07 to 2026-08-31, the 100 apps with the most tokens in the previous four weeks are carried forward for 8 weeks. Company dynamics (starting level, forward drift and survival by persistence class, damping, shock sizes and range width) are fitted at each cutoff from backtests whose targets end before it. After the cutoff the simulation receives only the published list prices (Internet Archive captures of the OpenRouter model list) and the dates new models were listed. The point prediction is the simulation's central path, with every random shock at its median; ranges are the 5th-95th percentile of 20 runs per cutoff. A test (`tests/test_market.py`) confirms that deleting all usage after a cutoff leaves the prediction unchanged.

Actual spend is each company's tokens times the blended list price of its use-case segment's actual model mix; a company's own model mix is not public. 19.1% of company-weeks after a cutoff fall outside OpenRouter's weekly top 200 and are recorded at half the 200th app's volume on both sides. Cutoffs four weeks apart give overlapping 8-week windows.

## Company-level accuracy

| Measure | Simulation | No change | Recent trend | Market growth |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Error in each company's 8-week tokens (token-weighted) | 44.2% | 45.1% | 65.8% | 53.1% |
| Error in each company's 8-week spend (spend-weighted) | 39.2% | 41.6% | 74.2% | 55.7% |
| Median company's error | 31.9% | 35.5% | 59.6% | 53.8% |
| Growth or decline called correctly (higher is better) | 66.9% | 64.9% | 65.4% | 61.2% |

The simulation's 90% range contained the actual 8-week tokens of 87.6% of companies.

## Totals

| Outcome | Simulation MAE | Best non-simulation MAE | Error reduction |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Tokens of the leading companies, 8 weeks (trillions) | 24.93 | 20.32 (Market growth) | -22.7% |
| Their spend at list prices, 8 weeks ($ millions) | 11.88 | 18.95 (No change) | 37.3% |
| All tokens on OpenRouter, 8 weeks (trillions) | 40.61 | 45.52 (Recent trend) | 10.8% |

Negative error reduction means the simulation loses.

## Limitations

This is a retrospective evaluation: the model's structure was chosen with these periods available, so it is not a prospective holdout. Forecasts published before the weeks they predict are the clean test. Usage covers traffic routed through OpenRouter, not the whole market; providers count tokens with different tokenizers; spend is at list prices, and caching and discounts make realised spend lower. The alternative's size depends on an authored price sensitivity (`calibration/market.yaml`).
