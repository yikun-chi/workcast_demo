# Validation: federal workforce separations after the 2025 changes (OPM)

Numeric claims are generated by `scripts/build_fedwork_validation.py` from `runs/fedwork/evaluation.json` and `instances/fedwork/calibration/selection.json`.

## Design

At each December cutoff every agency's employees become cohort twins (agency × age band × length of service × permanent appointment × full-time × supervisor; 2,312,280 employees at the held-out cutoff). Each twin's monthly separation rate by type is fitted on records effective and published by the cutoff. The world predicts each agency's separations in each month from 2025-02 to 2025-12 after the cutoff; the point prediction is the expected count averaged over 20 runs. Development cutoffs (2021-12, 2022-12, 2023-12) had no announced change and chose the specification; the 2024-12 cutoff is held out. For it, the return-to-office order and the deferred resignation offer enter on their announced dates with effects from earlier studies (`instances/fedwork/calibration/priors.yaml`). Agencies with fewer than 1,000 employees at the cutoff are scored as one group.

## Development selection

Rule: lowest mean over the development cutoffs of the error in each agency's monthly separations. 54 twin specifications were compared; the selected one (12-month rate window, shrinkage 1200, agency month pattern, depleting stock) had a mean development error of 19.25%. Exponential smoothing constants: alpha 0.1, beta 0.0, gamma 0.3, phi 0.9.

## Where the simulation loses

- In the development years without a policy change the simulation's error (19.3%) is higher than Exponential smoothing (18.3%).
- In 2025 the programme calculation, which applies the same published effects to each agency as a whole, is closer (40.9% against 46.3%).

## Alternative: no resignation offer

With the same 20 seeds, separations from 2025-02 to 2025-12 are 192,528 without the offer against 245,462 with it (-52,934; 90% range -64,369 to -39,979). This is a simulated alternative, not an observed estimate.

## Limitations

This is a retrospective evaluation: the design was chosen by people who knew the 2025 events in outline. The priors take published values as printed and the specification was chosen on development years only, but the policy channels themselves could not be tested before 2025. The simulation models only what was announced on 20-28 January 2025; reductions in force, later offers by individual agencies, the hiring freeze's effect on in-year hires and transfers, and probationary firings are not simulated (OPM withholds adverse-action codes, so most firings are also absent from the actual counts). OPM republishes files; this evaluation uses the pinned versions in `instances/fedwork/manifests/sources.json`, including revised versions of pre-2025 months. The frozen protocol is `docs/plans/fedwork_protocol.md`.
