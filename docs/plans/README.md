# Research protocols and source checks

These records document the designs and sources used by the instance and board
evaluations. They are retained at their original paths so existing citations and
provenance stay valid. Their dated observations, development results and original
machine paths are research context, not current setup instructions or deployment
authorization. For the supported workflow, use [setup](../setup.md).

| Record | Scope |
|---|---|
| [Ctrip](ctrip_protocol.md) | Call-centre output and attrition |
| [Federal workforce](fedwork_protocol.md) | Cohort separations and policy scenarios |
| [Census BTOS](btos_ai_protocol.md) | Business AI adoption |
| [Stack Overflow](so_chatgpt_protocol.md) | Question-volume displacement |
| [Economic Index](aei_tasks_protocol.md) | Occupation shares of AI use |
| [Buyer seat](buyer_seat_protocol.md) | Optional language-model purchasing policy |
| [Board methods](astra_forecast_protocol.md) | Statistical, simulation and language-model combinations |
| [Dataset feasibility](retrodiction_data_check.md) | Dated acquisition and licensing research for candidate datasets |

Use the [validation pages](../README.md#instance-evidence) for each implemented
instance's measured results and limitations. The [board guide](../../board/README.md)
distinguishes its component methods and publishing tools. Research records do not
override documented cutoff limitations or open review issues.
