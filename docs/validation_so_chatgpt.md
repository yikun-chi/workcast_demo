# Validation: A knowledge business after ChatGPT (Stack Overflow)

Numeric claims are generated by `scripts/build_adoption_validation.py` from `runs/so_chatgpt/evaluation.json`.

## Design

Rebuilds Stack Overflow's question flow as a population of would-be askers. Each week every asker who still posts questions may switch to an AI assistant, and some switch back, with hazards that follow a diffusion curve calibrated on the weeks before the cutoff; the questions that reach the site are the pre-ChatGPT trend and seasonality times the askers who have not switched. Predictions are scored against the questions actually posted in the 13 and 26 weeks after each cutoff, and from the launch week itself. The alternative asks how many questions the site would have kept if the switch to AI assistants had gone half as fast.

47 cutoffs from 2022-11-28 to 2026-07-06; the first 12 were used to make every design choice and the other 35 (2023-11-27 to 2026-07-06) were run once after the design was frozen in `docs/plans/so_chatgpt_protocol.md`. Targets run to 2026-10-19. Every prediction uses only periods before its cutoff (`tests/test_adoption.py` checks that deleting every later outcome leaves the prediction unchanged). The point prediction is the simulation's central path; ranges are the 5th-95th percentile of 20 runs per cutoff.

Counts come from the public Stack Exchange API and exclude deleted questions. Stack Overflow content is CC BY-SA; the API terms require attribution to the Stack Exchange network and bind users to its Terms of Service, which should be checked before commercial use. The official data dump (login and click-through since 2024) is not used.

## Alternative (simulated only)

A slower switch (for example, if the site had kept more askers with its own AI answers) halves the weekly probability that an asker who still posts on Stack Overflow moves to an AI assistant. The size of the change is authored, not estimated.

At the end of the horizon, averaged over the last 2 cutoffs and 20 seeds in the affected groups (stackoverflow): 56.10 questions as it happened in the simulation, 137.53 questions with the alternative. This is a simulated alternative, not an observed change.

## Limitations

- Deleted questions are missing, and older weeks have had longer to lose them.
- The no-AI baseline is fitted on the weeks before the launch available from the API's anonymous quota and carried forward for years; its trend and seasonality are an assumption, not an observation.
- One population for the whole site: tags, languages and question types are not modelled separately.
- The slower-switch alternative uses an authored hazard multiplier, not an estimated effect.
- Retrospective in part: the world type and the comparators were written with these series visible. The held-out cutoffs were run once after the design was frozen on the development cutoffs; no design choice was made on them.
