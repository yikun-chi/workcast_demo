# WORKCAST: proof before decisions

**Most organisations decide first and find out later.** A pilot takes months. A hiring plan is judged at year end. An AI budget is set by carrying last month forward. WORKCAST rebuilds an organisation as a simulation calibrated to its own data, so leaders can see the likely outcome of a decision before they commit to it.

**How we test ourselves.** Every claim below comes from a retrodiction: we take a real change whose outcome is on record, give the simulation only the data from before it, and score its prediction against what actually happened. We score the methods a planning team already uses on the same data, and fix the design in writing before the held-out periods are scored.

## What it means for a client

- **Decide in weeks, not quarters.** A simulation that reproduces a randomised trial's result before the trial runs turns one live pilot into a confirmation step instead of a search.
- **Fund the right size of change.** Borrowed effect sizes overstate benefits. Calibrating to the organisation's own record keeps the business case close to what is delivered.
- **Hire for the bottleneck, not the backlog.** The simulation separates where capacity limits throughput from where it does not.
- **Bring AI spend under control.** Halving forecast error on AI spend turns a volatile line item into one that can be committed in advance at a discount.

## The scientist's footnote

- **All results are backtests.** For the Ctrip, Census and Stack Overflow tests, the design was fixed in writing and the held-out periods were scored once. The Trip.com, purchase-to-pay and AI-spend tests are retrospective: their designs were settled with the outcome periods available.
- **The strongest tests had the hardest comparators.** The method was scored against trend, seasonal, adoption-curve and research-based benchmarks, not only "no change".
- **Some results are published as losses.** In a few further tests the simulation did not beat the strongest benchmark, and those results are published alongside these. Where WORKCAST wins most clearly is calibrated forecasting of operations, spend and adoption. Its digital-twin predictions of behaviour are proven on the Trip.com trial and are the next frontier.
- **Dollar figures are illustrations.** Each applies stated assumptions (salary, licence price, token spend) to an illustrative firm; a client's own figures replace them in scoping.
- **Every number traces to a sealed record.** Each figure links to its source file and calculation in the accompanying provenance record.
